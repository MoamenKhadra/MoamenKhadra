**Moamen Khadra** is a software engineer based in Alexandria, Egypt,
about to wrap up a Bachelor of Engineering in Electronics and
Communications at Alexandria University. He builds scalable APIs and
data-driven web applications, with a focus on performance, clean
architecture, and system design.

## What he does

- **Backend & APIs** — RESTful services in Python (Flask), Node.js
  (Express), and .NET, with MongoDB, PostgreSQL, and Redis behind them.
- **Frontend** — Angular and TypeScript interfaces that stay fast and
  accessible, styled with Tailwind CSS.
- **Delivery** — Docker, NGINX, and Linux: the boring, reliable parts
  that keep a product running in production.

## How he works

Two years of internships and a year-long, project-based software
engineering program taught him to favor boring correctness over clever
shortcuts: layered architecture, SOLID principles, and code a teammate
can read six months later. He has shipped a quiz platform backend, a
file-storage API with Redis caching, and a full-stack Airbnb clone
deployed behind NGINX.

Beyond the keyboard, he's a member of the EED Society's academic
committee at Alexandria University, where he's run workshops on
presentation and time management and managed one of the team's major
events.
