I’m **Moamen Khadra**, a freelance product engineer based in Alexandria,
Egypt. I help businesses turn complex services and day-to-day operations into
clear, dependable digital products — from websites that build trust
to web applications that give teams a better way to work.

I hold a Bachelor of Engineering in Electronics and Communications Engineering
from Alexandria University. My engineering background shapes how I approach
software: understand the system, make thoughtful trade-offs, and build a
solution people can rely on after launch.

## What I build

- **Business websites with a job to do** — fast, polished sites that explain a
  company’s value and make it easy for prospects to get in touch.
- **Tailored web applications** — dashboards, portals, and operational tools
  designed around real users, permissions, data, and business processes.
- **The foundations behind the interface** — secure REST APIs, databases,
  server-rendered pages, SEO, deployment, and working inquiry flows rather
  than a contact form that goes nowhere.

## How I work

I bring a product mindset to the technical work. That means starting with the
business goal, agreeing on a clear scope, and communicating directly throughout
the build. I care about the visible details — responsive interfaces, native RTL
layouts, and a clear message — as much as the less visible ones: clean
architecture, maintainable code, performance, and a reliable deployment.

My work spans polished public-facing websites and practical business systems,
using Angular, TypeScript, Python, .NET, PostgreSQL, Supabase, Docker, and
NGINX — chosen to fit the problem, not to chase a trend.

## Beyond the code

My early engineering experience was shaped by the ALX Software Engineering
Program, full-stack .NET internships, and the EED Society at Alexandria
University. Alongside technical work, I have led events and helped other
students develop presentation, research, and time-management skills. It made
me a clearer communicator and a more considerate collaborator.
