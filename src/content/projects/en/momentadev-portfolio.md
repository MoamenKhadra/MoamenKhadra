The site you are looking at right now. I wanted a personal home on the
internet that felt fast, worked in both English and Arabic, and didn't
depend on a pile of JavaScript frameworks.

## Why Astro

Static-first output means the pages ship as HTML with almost no client
JavaScript. Islands hydrate only where interactivity is actually needed —
the theme toggle, search, and language switcher.

## Highlights

- **Full RTL support** — the Arabic locale flips the layout with logical
  CSS properties rather than duplicated styles.
- **Automatic OG images** — every post gets a 1200×630 social card
  rendered at build time with Satori.
- **Instant search** — Pagefind indexes the static output, so search
  works offline of any backend.
